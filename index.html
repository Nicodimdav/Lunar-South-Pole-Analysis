Because the Moon is tidally locked, Earth hovers in a narrow sky zone. However, orbital eccentricity creates optical libration: Earth traces a ~15° × 13° figure-8 loop every 27.2–27.5 days. Near the poles, Earth sits low (0°–6°), meaning a local ridge can cause multi-day radio blackouts.

Vacuum & Sharp Horizon Cutoffs

With no atmosphere to refract light, horizon visibility is strictly geometric. If local LOLA topography rises above the Sun or Earth elevation minus apparent radius (0.27° for Sun, 0.95° for Earth), power and signal drop instantly to zero.

Why Schedule Slips Matter

A 2-week launch delay shifts the lunar phase by 180°, turning a sunlit landing site into pitch darkness. A 3-month slip alters the 1.54° seasonal tilt, plunging low ridges into deep winter shadows.
